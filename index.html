Our Story

A Small Kitchen With a Big Heart

Olive & Ember Kitchen started in 2012 as a family-run grill house with six tables and one wood-fired oven. Today we serve hundreds of guests every week — but every dish is still cooked the slow way, over real fire.

We buy vegetables, meat and seafood fresh each morning from local markets, bake our bread in-house, and never freeze anything that deserves to be fresh.
